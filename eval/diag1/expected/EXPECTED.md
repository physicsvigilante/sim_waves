# Frozen expectations (written before the teacher run; independent hand calculations)

## Echo hall
- **Baseline:** path difference 10 m; longest maximum lambda = 10 m.
- **E1, ceiling 30 m:** path difference 2*sqrt(60^2 + 30^2) - 120 = 60*sqrt(5) - 120 =
  14.164 m. Longest maximum lambda = 14.164 m. It increases.
- **E2, open boundary (pressure phase change pi):** maxima at 10 = (n - 1/2)*lambda.
  Longest = 20 m. The baseline's maxima (10, 5, ...) become minima.
- **E3, lambda = 10 m, listener at X:**
  - path difference d(X) = 2*sqrt((X/2)^2 + 625) - X;
  - relative intensity = 2 + 2*cos(2*pi*d/10), in amplitude^2 units with A = 1;
  - at X = 120 m, d = 10, intensity = 4 (a maximum).
  - The next maxima are where d = 10*n; d decreases as X grows. d = 10 is the only
    maximum between 100 and 140 m: d(100) = 11.80 and d(140) = 8.6.
- **E4:** a plot of E3's intensity vs X over 100–140 m. The label must say relative
  (amplitude^2) units.

## Pipe (closed-open, L = 0.85 m, v = 340 m/s, e = 0)
- **Baseline:** f1 = 100 Hz, f3 = 300 Hz.
- **P1, v = 350:** f1 = 350/(4*0.85) = 102.94 Hz.
- **P2, open-open:** f1 = 340/(2*0.85) = 200 Hz; a 2nd harmonic exists (400 Hz).
- **P3, 3rd harmonic:** lambda = 4*0.85/3 = 1.1333 m.
  - Displacement nodes at x = 0 and 0.5667 m.
  - Pressure nodes at 0.2833 and 0.85 m.
  - With 500 Hz, lambda = 0.68 m, resonant lengths are (2n-1)*0.17 = 0.17, 0.51, 0.85,
    1.19 m. In [0.2, 1.2] that is 0.51, 0.85 and 1.19 m (3 lengths).
- **P4:** the profile |sin(2*pi*x/1.1333)| shape: zero at 0 and 0.5667 m, maxima at
  0.2833 and 0.85 m.

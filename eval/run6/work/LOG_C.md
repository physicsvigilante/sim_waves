## Three most common friction points
1. Quantities that are not observables: pressure as P0 +/- amplitude, speed of sound relative to a moving passenger, heat exchange, intensity on moving rays (refused), Hooke tension from strain. I had to bridge by hand or improvise (f*wavelength).
2. Unstated or figure-only inputs: figure shapes/scales had to be invented as assumptions (comprehension_12), and ranged or scaled arbitrary values can break scenes (echo existence in jee_main_47, a NOT AVAILABLE comparison in reasoning_9), so I fixed numbers as assumptions.
3. Ambiguous or flawed problems (fundamental not stated in single_102, matrix_2 omega has no match, jee_advanced_54 speed wording) and comparing two setups forces duplicating media/sources (jee_advanced_37 wind directions).

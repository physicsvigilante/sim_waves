## Three most common friction points
1. Underdetermined scale: strings and resonators need a mu, length, tension or amplitude the problem never gives, so I had to invent assumed scale values (and facts) only to run a ratio question.
2. Things the engine refuses or lacks: sum of 3 frequencies (beats), intensity() of a sum, cot/non-sinusoidal standing profile, damping, Hooke's-law stretching, particle KE of a mode over time.
3. Small glue by hand: choosing the "next" harmonic from a list (no indexing), unit conversions such as K to degC and kgf, and comparing the engine value to the option expression; reserved names (mu) also tripped v1.
